How to use the LoveGoBuy spreadsheet

Start with a CAFANS product page and check whether LoveGoBuy appears under its buying options. Confirm the exact listing and variant on the destination platform before paying.

What to check before ordering

  1. Shoes. Save the size chart for the selected variant. Check whether the chart measures the foot or the insole, and compare the color and size label in any warehouse photos.
  2. Tees and hoodies. Lay a similar garment flat and measure chest width and body length. Confirm whether the seller lists a flat width or a full circumference.
  3. Jackets. Compare shoulder width and sleeve length with a jacket you wear over the same layers. Use available QC photos to inspect the zipper, cuffs and lining.
  4. Shipping. Request a quote for your destination. Check how the selected line calculates billable weight and whether any taxes or fees are already included.

Frequently asked questions

What is this LoveGoBuy spreadsheet?

It is a browsable shortlist of CAFANS product finds with links to the corresponding product pages. You can use it in your browser without downloading a spreadsheet.

Does the displayed price include international shipping?

The displayed USD price is an item estimate. Get a destination-specific shipping quote and include any applicable local delivery charges, taxes or fees when comparing totals.

Where can I find QC photos for my order?

Check the warehouse or order section of the shopping agent you used. If photos are available, review the exact variant, labels, measurements and visible condition before approving shipment.

What if a product link or variant is unavailable?

Return to the CAFANS catalog and search for a comparable item. Check the replacement listing’s measurements, seller terms and shipping eligibility before ordering.
